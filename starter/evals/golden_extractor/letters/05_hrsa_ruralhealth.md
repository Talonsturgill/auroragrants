# U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Health Resources and Services Administration

Notice of Award

April 05, 2026

Dear Authorized Official,

The Health Resources and Services Administration is pleased to notify you that your organization has been selected for funding under the Rural Health Care Services Outreach Program, CFDA 93.912, in the amount of $750,000. The period of performance is 3 years, beginning May 1, 2026 and ending April 30, 2029. This award is governed by 45 CFR 75 and the program-specific terms set forth in the attached notice of award.

## Reporting Requirements

The following reports are required over the life of the award.

Annual Programmatic Performance Report. Your organization must submit an annual Programmatic Performance Report (PPR) covering each 12-month budget period. Each annual PPR is due no later than 90 days after the end of the budget period. Reports are submitted through the HRSA Electronic Handbooks (EHB) portal. Each annual PPR must include the following narrative sections.
- Project Progress. A description of project activities, milestones reached, and work completed during the budget period. Word count cap of 1,500 words.
- Performance Measures. Quantitative results for each performance measure listed in the approved work plan, with variance explanations as needed. Word count cap of 1,000 words.
- Collaboration and Network Activities. A summary of consortium partner contributions and collaborative activities during the reporting period. Word count cap of 800 words.

Semi-Annual Federal Financial Report (SF-425). Your organization must submit the SF-425 on a semi-annual basis. Each SF-425 covers a six-month reporting period and is due no later than 30 days after the close of the reporting period. SF-425 submissions are handled through the Payment Management System.

Final Comprehensive Report. A final comprehensive report is due no later than 120 days after the end of the period of performance. The final report is submitted through the HRSA Electronic Handbooks portal. The final comprehensive report must include the following narrative sections.
- Cumulative Project Outcomes. Word count cap of 2,000 words.
- Sustainability Plan. Word count cap of 1,000 words.
- Dissemination of Findings. Word count cap of 750 words.

## Drawdowns and Compliance

Funds will be drawn through the Payment Management System based on actual costs incurred. All expenditures must conform to 45 CFR 75 and HRSA-specific program terms. Any change in key personnel or project scope of 25 percent or more requires prior approval from the HRSA Project Officer.

## Conditions

This award is subject to any terms and conditions specified in the attached Notice of Award. Failure to meet reporting deadlines may result in enforcement actions up to and including termination.

Please contact your HRSA Grants Management Specialist with any questions about the financial or administrative terms of this award.
