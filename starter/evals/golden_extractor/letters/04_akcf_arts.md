# Alaska Community Foundation

April 12, 2026

Dear Grantee,

Thank you for your application to the Alaska Community Foundation's Arts Project Fund. On behalf of the Grants Committee, I am pleased to inform you that your organization has been awarded $25,000 in support of the arts project described in your proposal. The grant period is 6 months, beginning May 1, 2026 and ending October 31, 2026.

## Reporting Requirements

The Foundation requires one final report for this grant. The final report is due no later than 60 days after the end of the grant period. Reports are submitted through the Foundation's online grantee portal. The final report must include the following narrative sections.

1. Project Description. A short description of the arts programming completed under the grant, including venues, artists, and community partners. Word count cap of 500 words.
2. Community Reach. A description of audience, participants, and communities reached during the project period, including approximate numbers and demographic notes where available. Word count cap of 400 words.
3. Budget Narrative. A plain-language summary of expenditures against the approved budget. Word count cap of 300 words.
4. Artist and Community Reflections. Brief reflections from participating artists or community members on the project's value and outcomes. Word count cap of 400 words.

No interim reports are required. The Foundation encourages informal email updates and invites grantees to share photos and stories through our monthly newsletter with appropriate releases.

## Acknowledgment and Public Materials

Please credit the Alaska Community Foundation in event programs, signage, press releases, and digital posts connected to the funded work. Approved logos and suggested credit lines are available on the Foundation's website.

## Use of Funds

Funds must be used in alignment with the project scope and budget as approved. Material changes to scope, schedule, or budget require prior written approval from the Foundation. Unspent funds at the end of the grant period must be returned to the Foundation within 30 days of the report submission.

## Contact

Your assigned program officer will reach out within two weeks to schedule a brief check-in. If you have questions, please reach out by phone or email to the Foundation office in Anchorage.

We look forward to seeing the project come to life and are grateful for your contribution to Alaska's arts landscape.

Sincerely,

Nina Kemppel
