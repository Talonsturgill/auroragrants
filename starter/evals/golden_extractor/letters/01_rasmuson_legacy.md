# Rasmuson Foundation

April 14, 2026

Dear Grantee,

On behalf of the Rasmuson Foundation Board of Trustees, I am pleased to inform you that your organization has been awarded a grant under the Legacy Grant program in the amount of $100,000. The award period runs from May 1, 2026 through April 30, 2027, a period of 12 months. Funds are for the project described in your application and may not be redirected without prior written approval from the Foundation.

## Reporting Requirements

Your organization is required to submit one final narrative report at the close of the grant period. The final narrative report is due no later than 90 days after the end of the award period. Reports submitted after that date without an approved extension will be considered non-compliant and may affect future eligibility.

The final narrative report must be submitted through the Foundation's online grantee portal. The portal accepts text entries and supporting documents. Please plan to include the following narrative sections in your final report.

1. Project Summary. A concise overview of the work completed during the grant period. Word count cap of 500 words.
2. Outcomes and Impact. A description of measured outcomes against the goals stated in your application, including any quantitative indicators. Word count cap of 750 words.
3. Financial Summary. A plain-language description of how funds were expended, aligned with your approved budget. Word count cap of 400 words.
4. Lessons Learned. Reflections on what worked, what did not, and what the organization would change for future work. Word count cap of 400 words.

The Foundation does not require interim progress reports for Legacy Grants of this size. However, we do welcome informal updates by email at any time, and your program officer remains available to discuss any material changes to scope or budget.

## Use of Funds

Funds are restricted to the project described in your proposal. Any reallocation of more than 15 percent between approved budget categories requires prior written approval. Equipment purchases over $5,000 must be reported with a short justification at the time of the final report.

## Acknowledgment

Please acknowledge the Rasmuson Foundation in public-facing materials connected to this project, using the language and logo guidance on our website.

We look forward to hearing about your work. If you have any questions about reporting, please contact your program officer at the Foundation office in Anchorage.
