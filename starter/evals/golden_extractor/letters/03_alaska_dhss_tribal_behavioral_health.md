# State of Alaska, Department of Health and Social Services

Grant Award Notification

April 08, 2026

Dear Grantee,

The Alaska Department of Health and Social Services, Division of Behavioral Health, is pleased to notify you that your organization has been awarded funding under the Tribal Behavioral Health program in the amount of $500,000. The award period spans 36 months, beginning May 1, 2026 and ending April 30, 2029. This grant supports community-based behavioral health services in tribal and rural Alaska.

## Reporting Requirements

The Department requires two recurring reports over the life of this award.

Annual Progress Report. Your organization must submit an annual progress report covering each 12-month reporting period of performance. The annual progress report is due no later than 60 days after the end of each reporting period. Reports are submitted through the DHSS grantee portal. Each annual progress report must include the following narrative sections.
- Program Activities. A description of behavioral health services delivered during the reporting year, including service modalities and community engagement activities. Word count cap of 1,200 words.
- Client Outcomes. Aggregated outcomes for clients served during the year, including any standardized assessment measures and qualitative indicators. Word count cap of 1,000 words.
- Workforce and Capacity. A summary of staff hired, trained, or retained under the award. Word count cap of 600 words.
- Cultural Responsiveness. A description of how services were adapted to the cultural context of the communities served. Word count cap of 600 words.

Annual Financial Report. Your organization must also submit an annual financial report covering the same 12-month reporting period. The annual financial report is due no later than 60 days after the end of each reporting period. The financial report is submitted through the DHSS grantee portal and aligns to the approved budget categories.

## Match and Allowable Costs

This award requires no match. Allowable costs are defined by the attached budget and by applicable state and federal cost principles. Any change of scope or reallocation exceeding 10 percent of a budget line requires prior written approval.

## Termination and Non-Compliance

Failure to submit required reports by the stated deadlines may result in withholding of funds or suspension of the award. The Department may terminate the award for cause following written notice and an opportunity to cure.

Please sign and return the award acceptance form within 14 calendar days. If you have questions, contact your assigned grants manager in Juneau.
