# U.S. Department of the Interior, Bureau of Indian Affairs

Notice of Award

April 10, 2026

Dear Authorized Representative,

This letter constitutes formal notice that the Bureau of Indian Affairs has awarded your tribal organization funding under the Tribal Youth Initiative, CFDA 15.141, in the amount of $250,000. The period of performance is 24 months, beginning May 15, 2026 and ending May 14, 2028. This award is subject to 2 CFR 200 and the additional terms and conditions set forth in the attached award document.

## Reporting Requirements

The Bureau requires the following reports over the life of the award.

Quarterly Programmatic Performance Reports. Your organization must submit a Programmatic Performance Report each quarter covering the prior three-month reporting period. Each PPR is due no later than 30 days after the close of the reporting period. Reports are submitted through the GrantSolutions portal. Each PPR must contain the following narrative sections.
- Activities Completed. A description of youth programming delivered during the quarter. Word count cap of 600 words.
- Participant Outcomes. Quantitative and qualitative results for youth participants, including any culturally grounded indicators. Word count cap of 600 words.
- Challenges and Mitigations. A short account of obstacles encountered and steps taken to address them. Word count cap of 400 words.

Final Narrative Report. A comprehensive final narrative report is due 120 days after the end of the period of performance. The final report must include a full cumulative summary of activities, outcomes, participant numbers, and sustainability plans. It is submitted through GrantSolutions. The final narrative must contain the following sections.
- Executive Summary. Word count cap of 500 words.
- Cumulative Outcomes. Word count cap of 1,500 words.
- Sustainability and Next Steps. Word count cap of 750 words.

SF-425 Federal Financial Reports. Your organization must submit the SF-425 on a semi-annual basis. Each SF-425 covers a six-month reporting period and is due no later than 30 days after the close of the period. SF-425 submissions are handled through Grants.gov.

## Drawdowns and Payments

Funds will be disbursed through the Automated Standard Application for Payments system. Drawdowns must align with actual project costs and cannot exceed the approved budget by category without prior written approval.

## Compliance

This award is governed by 25 U.S.C. 13 and applicable federal regulations. Any subaward must be cleared through the assigned program officer. The Bureau reserves the right to audit project records for the duration of the performance period and for three years thereafter.

Please confirm receipt of this award within ten business days by returning the signed acceptance page.
